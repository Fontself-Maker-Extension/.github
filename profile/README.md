## 🚀 Overview
Fontself Maker for Windows is a panel-based extension that lives inside Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, letting you convert your own vector or raster lettering into a working font without leaving the app you already design in. Instead of jumping between separate font editors, you select your letters, drag them into the Fontself panel, and the extension assembles them into a complete OpenType file you can install and type with anywhere. As a Fontself Maker Illustrator plugin and Photoshop plugin, it is aimed at type designers, lettering artists, and creatives who want to go from sketch to shareable font in a single, familiar workflow.

## ✨ Features
- **Drag-and-drop font building** — select your glyphs and drop them into the panel to map them to characters automatically.
- **Works in both Adobe apps** — the same extension runs inside Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Photoshop, so you can use vectors or raster artwork.
- **OpenType export** — save a standard OpenType (.otf) font that installs on Windows and works across your other applications.
- **Advanced spacing and kerning** — fine-tune letter spacing, kerning pairs, and ligatures directly from the panel.
- **Color font support** — build layered color and SVG fonts for expressive, multi-tone lettering.

## 📥 Installation
1. Click the **Download** button above to get the latest Fontself Maker extension package.
2. Run the downloaded installer, or add the extension through your Adobe extension manager, and follow the on-screen steps.
3. Open Adobe Illustrator or Adobe Photoshop — Fontself Maker appears as a panel in the Window menu, ready to activate with your license or free trial.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop and create or open a project containing your lettering.
2. Open the Fontself Maker panel from the Window menu.
3. Select the letters you want to include and drag them into the panel to map them to characters.
4. Adjust spacing, kerning, and any color layers to taste.
5. Click **Save** to export your OpenType font, then install it on Windows and start typing.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is Fontself Maker free?**
Fontself Maker is a paid extension, but you can try it with a free trial/demo so you can test building a font inside Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop before buying a license. Searches for a "fontself maker free download" lead to that trial rather than a free full copy.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
Fontself Maker runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit), as long as you have a compatible, recent version of Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop installed to host the extension.

**Is my data secure?**
Your artwork and fonts are created and saved locally on your own Windows machine. Fontself Maker works within your Adobe app, so your source files and exported font files stay under your control.
